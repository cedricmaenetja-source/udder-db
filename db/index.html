I'm a vendor and noticed incorrect information. What should I do?
+

If you are a vendor and notice any incorrect or outdated information listed on our platform, you can create a vendor account and claim your profile. Once verified, you'll be able to update, edit, and manage your company information to ensure it remains accurate and up to date.

Creating an account also gives you access to additional features such as profile enhancements, analytics insights, and direct customer engagement tools.

How can I trust the accuracy of the data if it is AI-generated?
+

Our AI system analyzes structured data, trusted sources, and verified vendor information to generate insights and recommendations. While AI helps organize and enhance information efficiently, it does not replace human verification.

We continuously refine our models, validate data where possible, and allow vendors to claim and update their profiles to improve accuracy. We also encourage users to verify critical business decisions directly with vendors.
