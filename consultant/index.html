Alice Oduya · 3 days ago · Tag: Negotiation
Sales cycle is slow (avg 9 months enterprise). They heavily discount on multi-year deals — always push for 3yr commitment to unlock 20–25% off list. Watch for "success plan" add-on; it's usually negotiable to free.
Rachel Patel · 2 weeks ago · Tag: Implementation
Complex global rollouts take 6–9 months. CRM module lags behind core ATS. Be wary of co-selling with their integration partners — they push towards bespoke consulting rather than leveraging Udder expertise.
Tom Fielding · Nov 2025 · Tag: Cultural Fit
Strong cultural fit with people-first orgs and those undergoing global HR transformation. Less good for SMBs or orgs wanting out-of-the-box simplicity — they will over-configure.
